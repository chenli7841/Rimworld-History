# HR-012 implementation status: factions and prototype geography

## Implemented slice

- Nine HR-004 historical states have separate RimWorld `FactionDef` definitions and HR state bindings: Eastern Zhou, Zheng, Jin, Qin, Qi, Lu, Song, Wey (Wei), and Chu. Jin remains a single polity; no Warring States successors are introduced.
- The historical faction-generation path activates only when the selected scenario setup is `HR_Scenario_EasternZhou_770BCE`. Harmony filters the configurable world factions and the faction list passed to `WorldGenerator.GenerateWorld`; other scenarios keep the ordinary faction list.
- A runtime `FactionManager.GetFactions` filter limits general faction queries in this scenario to the nine bound factions and the hidden local-bandit faction. This gives systems that use this API a historical faction pool without changing RimWorld's faction classes.
- The world component stores generation mode, scenario, faction IDs, generated settlement placements, and prototype tile-to-region bindings with Scribe. It is absent from ordinary scenarios and existing saves are not rewritten.
- Nine placement Defs define relative longitude/latitude offsets from one generated-world anchor (Luoyi is the anchor; the other eight positions are relative). The controller moves generated settlements to nearby passable tiles, associates a small surrounding tile area with the prototype region, and requires the Zheng region for the player's starting site. If required placements fail, the starting-site page cannot continue.
- After placement, the generated RimWorld settlements receive localized names from their matching historical settlement Defs (Luoyi and prototype regional labels) instead of keeping random settlement names. The game-generated terrain and biome labels remain procedural.
- A hidden `HR_Faction_LocalBandits` Def supplies a historical-era tribal combat source without appearing as a selectable state. Faction queries and world generation include it only in the Eastern Zhou path.
- The nine states start with neutral RimWorld relations. HR-004 political setup carries the initial nominal-suzerainty relationships separately. The adapter does not equate nominal overlordship with an invented RimWorld goodwill score.
- Custom human PawnKinds use tribal/neolithic equipment. Three trader kinds offer basic food, clothing, herbal medicine, wood, and the mod's historical materials; buy lists whitelist only those goods.

## Spatial model and limits

This is a **relative prototype layout**, not a real map of China or a verified reconstruction of BCE borders. Region offsets are game design coordinates around RimWorld's generated globe, while their labels describe intended relative placement. Tiles close to the anchor are assigned to each prototype region; no Earth projection or historical polygon dataset is included. All nine settlements are required, so small or unsuitable generated worlds may fail the placement check rather than silently claim a valid historical layout.

The map continues to use RimWorld's normal world generator. HR-012 does not add a RealEarth map, terrain reconstruction, special rivers, or historically surveyed border geometry. The settlement positions and the four-tile region association are approximate game data, not evidence about precise ancient boundaries.

## Filtering and behavior limits

Faction generation is scenario-scoped. The general `GetFactions` query filter covers consumers that use that RimWorld API, but the implementation does not audit or patch every direct `Faction.OfMechanoids`, insect, quest, incident, or DLC-specific faction source. It also does not suppress every vanilla incident/quest by itself. No DLC beyond the existing Biotech requirement is referenced by the new PawnKinds or trader definitions.

The hidden bandit faction is a single generic hostile faction source. It does not yet provide regional bandit identities, custom incident scheduling, or a dedicated raid AI. Historical polity relations are not continuously synchronized into vanilla goodwill, alliances, or war declarations; HR-004 remains the separate source of political state.
